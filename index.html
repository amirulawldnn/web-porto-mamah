Philosophy & Values

About My Teaching

"Mathematics is not a spectator sport. It is an active journey of curiosity, discovery, and resilience."
— Bu Khusnul Khotimah

My mission as a middle school mathematics educator is to create a classroom where students feel safe to ask questions, to make mistakes, and to persist through difficulty. I believe that every student is capable of mathematical thinking — the role of a teacher is to help them believe that too.

Mission

To make mathematics accessible, relevant, and genuinely inspiring for every student.

Approach

Visual representations, hands-on activities, and grounding abstract ideas in real-world context.

Values

Patience, encouragement, a growth mindset, and the belief that curiosity is a skill to be cultivated.
